Da Ebony Warriors Society
Hardcoded website

Open index.html in any browser to look at the site.

To put it on GoDaddy:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open your hosting file manager and go to public_html.
3. Upload the files inside this folder (the html pages, css, js, images, and favicon). Do not upload this READ-ME if you do not want it public.
4. Your domain should show index.html as the home page.

Pages: index.html, about.html, work.html, gatherings.html, contact.html.

Longer paragraphs are still lorem ipsum. Send documents and images when you have them and they can be added.

The contact form does not email anyone. It only shows the note on the page. Instagram (@daebonyworriors) is the live way to reach the society.
